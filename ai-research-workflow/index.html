AI4Math

An open workflow for AI-assisted mathematics, with explicit evidence, independent review, and human decisions.

Research with AI.
Keep the proof in view.

AI Research Workflow keeps theorem targets, evidence, independent review, and unresolved gaps visible across research sessions. The researcher retains the decision to accept a result.

Version 2.7.0 · MIT licensed · Local, inspectable research records

01Keep the question precise.

Separate the theorem target, its necessary lemmas, and the route being tested. Make assumptions and the stopping condition explicit.

02Let evidence keep its scope.

Distinguish a proof, a finite check, a numerical observation, and an external result. Retain failed candidates and exactly what they exclude.

03Review before accepting.

Use separate Director, Researcher, and Critic tasks. Independent review informs the decision; explicit owner approval changes the accepted record.

Scope and current limits

The supported core is mathematical research. An experimental AI4S profile adds data provenance, controls, frozen evaluation, and reproducibility. The project does not include a foundation model, an automatic theorem prover, or a complete autonomous scientist. Workflow consistency checks do not certify mathematical truth, and comparative productivity gains have not yet been established.

For a selected manuscript, Math Paper Editor adds scoped review and traceable revision. Its usage demonstration shows what a language edit may change.
