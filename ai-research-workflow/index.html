Personal notes · Workflow practice

01Keep working notes usable.

Keep the question, assumptions, dependencies, and unfinished steps together so I can pick up the thread later.

02Keep checks in context.

Label calculations, numerical observations, external inputs, and proofs clearly in my notes, including what remains unverified.

03Keep revisions visible.

Retain the original text and the diff, distinguish language edits from mathematical changes, and review suggestions before accepting them.

Workflow note 02 · Editing

Reviewing and revising drafts

Math Paper Editor packages instructions and helpers from my manuscript-editing workflow. It supports scoped review and conservative LaTeX revision, separates mathematical concerns from language edits, and keeps source changes visible.

The current version supports read-only review, conservative editing, deeper proof inspection, and authorized restructuring of existing arguments. Its demonstration shows what “edit only the Introduction” allows, including a valid zero-change outcome.
